01

Introduction

AU Connect ("we", "our", or "us") is a social networking platform designed to connect students, alumni, professors, and other members of the Assumption University community.

This Privacy Policy explains how AU Connect collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our platform.

By using AU Connect, you acknowledge that your information will be handled as described in this policy.

02

Information we collect

We collect information necessary to provide and maintain AU Connect's features.

Account information

When you create an account or sign in, we may collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your profile picture.
  • Your account identifier from the authentication provider.
  • Your authentication and session information.

AU Connect supports third-party authentication providers. The information received may vary depending on the provider you choose.

Profile information

You may choose to provide additional information for your AU Connect profile, including:

  • Academic and professional background.
  • Biography and personal introduction.
  • Skills, interests, and areas of expertise.
  • Other profile details supported by the platform.

Information you add to your profile may be visible to other users, depending on the platform's visibility settings and features.

User-generated content

When you use AU Connect, we may collect and store content you submit, including:

  • Posts, articles, and comments.
  • Images and videos.
  • Documents and other supported attachments.
  • Job postings and professional opportunities.
  • Other content and information you choose to share through the platform.

Content you publish may be accessible to other users. You should review the information you share before publishing it.

Usage and technical information

We may process technical information necessary to operate, maintain, and secure AU Connect, including authentication sessions, request information, and diagnostic or error information.

The specific information collected depends on how you use the platform and the technical services involved.

03

How we use your information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Account management: Create, maintain, and manage your account.
  • Authentication: Verify your identity and maintain secure sign-in sessions.
  • Platform functionality: Display profiles, posts, comments, and other content you submit.
  • Networking: Help users discover and connect with members of the AU community.
  • Recommendations: Support features that suggest potentially relevant users or professional connections.
  • Professional opportunities: Enable users to share and discover jobs and other career-related content.
  • Platform security: Detect misuse, investigate technical issues, and protect accounts and services.
  • Maintenance and improvement: Diagnose problems and improve platform functionality.

We aim to use personal information only for purposes relevant to operating and improving AU Connect.

04

Publicly visible information and content

AU Connect is designed to facilitate communication and networking within the university community.

Depending on the platform's features and settings, information such as your name, profile picture, profile details, posts, comments, and other content you publish may be visible to other users.

Please avoid publishing sensitive personal information or information belonging to other people without their permission.

We cannot guarantee that other users will not copy, save, or redistribute information that they can access through the platform.

05

Third-party services

AU Connect relies on third-party services to support certain platform features. These services may include:

  • Authentication providers: Used to authenticate users and facilitate account access.
  • Cloud infrastructure and databases: Used to host the application and store account information and platform content.
  • Media storage services: Used to store and deliver supported images, videos, and other uploaded files.
  • Recommendation services: Used to process relevant profile information to provide user recommendations.

These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective functions.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Their involvement does not mean that AU Connect endorses every aspect of their practices.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of relevant third-party services.

06

Data storage and security

AU Connect uses databases, cloud infrastructure, and other technical services to store and process information required to operate the platform.

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no system can guarantee absolute security. We cannot promise that information transmitted or stored electronically will always remain secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their accounts and avoiding the disclosure of authentication credentials or sensitive information.

07

Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell users' personal information.

We may process or disclose information in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: To third-party providers that support the operation, hosting, storage, authentication, or maintenance of AU Connect.
  • Platform visibility: When you publish information or content that other users are permitted to access.
  • Security and legal obligations: When disclosure is reasonably necessary to address security incidents, enforce applicable rules, or comply with applicable legal requirements.
  • With your direction or consent: When you authorize a particular disclosure or use of your information.

We seek to limit disclosures to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

08

Data retention and account deletion

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the platform, maintain accounts, meet operational requirements, and address applicable legal or security obligations.

If account deletion is available, users may use the designated account-management process to request deletion of their account and associated information.

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where necessary for security, legal compliance, or legitimate operational purposes.

Deleting an account or content may not immediately remove every copy from backups, logs, or systems maintained by third-party service providers.

09

User rights and choices

Users can control certain information by choosing what to include in their profiles and what content to publish.

Depending on applicable law and the platform's available features, users may have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Object to or request restrictions on certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Requests concerning personal information can be directed to the contact address below.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. Some requests may be subject to applicable legal exceptions.

10

Children's privacy

AU Connect is intended for members of the university community and other eligible users.

The platform is not intended to be used in violation of applicable age requirements or privacy laws. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children where doing so would violate applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that the situation can be reviewed.

11

International data processing

AU Connect uses third-party infrastructure and services to operate the platform. Depending on the providers and their configurations, personal information may be stored or processed in countries other than the country where a user resides.

Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to ensure that personal information is handled in accordance with relevant privacy requirements.

12

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as AU Connect evolves, introduces new features, or changes how personal information is processed.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.

Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how their information is handled.

13

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to raise a privacy concern, or want to make a request concerning your personal information, please contact us.
